Methodology

Definitions to keep the dashboard interpretable and trustworthy.

Total Revenue

Sum of sales after discount across the selected records.

Average Order Value

Total revenue divided by distinct order count.

Average Discount

Simple average of discount percentages in the selected records.

Contribution

Share of total filtered revenue attributed to a category, city, product, or customer.
